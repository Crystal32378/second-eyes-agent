# Shortlist brief — input spec (v-next, NOT yet consumed by any runner)

Positioning: 400 photos (404 real source files scanned, presented as a
400-photo demo scenario), four hours to deadline. Second Eyes delivers a
shortlist per this brief, each candidate with reasons + pending items.
Humans pick the final three by instinct; the machine never ranks heroes
and never pretends taste.

## Boundary (standing)

No brief = no clearance basis. Without a brief, every photo is 待確認
at best — never "ready to publish". Second Eyes checks ONLY what the
brief states; it endorses no contract, licence, or legal status.

## What a brief contains (explicit rules only)

- Required scenes / slots (e.g. 主圖1, 情境2, 細節1) → drives Coverage
- Hard constraints (e.g. no people, min resolution, channel sizes)
- Known facts the editor supplies (e.g. SKUs live, rights cleared list)
- Everything NOT in the brief is uncheckable → 待確認, never Ready

## What the machine does (only three things)

1. Read the explicit rules in the brief.
2. Exclude obvious mismatches and evidence-less photos.
3. Deliver a reasoned, traceable shortlist (10–20 candidates max).

## Explicitly out of scope (never)

- Absolute colour correctness, complete product taxonomy
- Taste / hero ranking / final publish decision
- Inferring portrait or licence status from pixels

## Coverage

Per required scene: HAVE (count) / MISSING. Missing scenes are reported,
never filled by guessing.
