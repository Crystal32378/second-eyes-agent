# Publish-risk decision tree — wired vs v-next (honesty record)

Owner's order, for a social editor handed a hard drive asking
"我今天現在能先發哪幾張？為什麼？" Three baskets answer that question;
CLEAR/CONFLICT/NEW/UNKNOWN stay INTERNAL evidence states (see README).

## The four steps and their connection status

1. **有沒有人物** — NOT WIRED. Personnel is excluded from scope entirely
   (Batch A is non-Personnel by membership). Portrait/privacy/licence risk
   is therefore OUTSIDE the machine's vision; any file with a person goes
   to a human by policy, never by model judgment. V-next: person detection
   as a Hold trigger (detection only, never identification).

2. **有沒有清楚產品** — WIRED (frozen vocab item + subject definition).
   No/cut/unrecognizable product cannot CLEAR; unlabelled + observed goes
   NEW (needs first human look), never Ready on pixels alone.

3. **畫面是否可直接使用** — HALF-WIRED. Framing/crop is partially covered
   (shot field); resolution, blur, watermark, text errors, clutter and
   generation artifacts have NO connected source. V-next.

4. **是否符合已知發布條件** — NOT WIRED. Channel sizes, product info,
   brand rules, source/licence status have NO connected source
   (DATA-CONTRACT: source_unconnected). V-next: connect rights sources
   before any "cleared" claim beyond label agreement.

## Rule that follows

A basket is only as strong as its weakest connected step. Today only step
2 (and part of 3) is wired — so Ready candidate means "no LABEL conflict
detected", never "cleared for publish". The machine's most valuable output
remains the other two baskets: exactly what is missing, and who holds it.
